## Generated environment

Saved asset: **`public/horror/corridor.png`** (1536 × 1024).

Generated using the built-in **image_gen** tool and the **imagegen** skill. No CLI fallback or external API key was used. The output was inspected and copied into the workspace.

Exact final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background image for an interactive analog-horror surveillance game, landscape 1536x1024. Generate one cinematic photoreal abandoned psychiatric hospital corridor seen by a security camera at human eye height: long oppressive decaying corridor, wet cracked concrete, peeling moldy plaster, rusty doorways on both sides, an abandoned wheelchair and small stained child's doll seated on it on the left, broken overhead fluorescent tubes emitting faint sickly green light, weak red emergency lamp at the far doorway, thick shadows. At the very end a barely visible unnaturally tall faceless silhouette. Found-footage survival horror, realistic film production photograph, detailed surfaces, subtle dust and haze, ominous dread, not a cartoon or illustration. Image should be dark and atmospheric but corridor and objects clearly readable, not crushed blacks. Strong centered vanishing point, wide scene. No text, lettering, logos, watermarks, borders or interface; no gore. This will replace a too-friendly vector room in a horror game.

The scene combines this environment with the supplied photographs, different camera crops, per-chapter color treatment, noise lines, darkness, reveal timing, and pursuit overlays. It does not claim to contain six independently photographed rooms.
